## Program Overview

### Description

The Amity Online MCA is a **2-year (4 semesters) postgraduate program** meticulously crafted to prepare students for the evolving IT sector. The curriculum emphasizes both foundational and advanced programming constructs, application modeling, and the latest industry-relevant technologies. The program is designed for a diverse audience, including fresh graduates, working professionals, and those seeking to pivot into IT from other domains.

### Key Highlights

- **Duration:** 2 years (4 semesters)
- **Total Credits:** 103
- **Mode:** 100% Online (Live + Recorded lectures, LMS access, mobile app support)
- **Specializations:** Blockchain, Machine Learning & Artificial Intelligence, Augmented Reality & Virtual Reality, Cybersecurity, Software Engineering, Financial Technology & AI
- **Faculty:** Renowned Indian and International faculty
- **Learning Model:** 350+ hours of video lectures, robust LMS, flipped learning methodology
- **Industry Collaborations:** Specializations in collaboration with TCS iON and other industry partners
- **Real-World Projects:** Integrated case studies and capstone projects
- **Internships:** Curated opportunities with leading companies
- **Career Support:** Resume building, mock interviews, placement assistance with 500+ hiring partners
- **Tech Tools:** Amigo App (LMS), Prof. Ami (AI tutor), beSocial (student community platform)
- **Global Recognition:** WASC (USA), QAA (UK), WES (Canada & USA), QS Rankings, NAAC A+

### Learning Outcomes

Graduates of the program are expected to:

- Master programming languages and application development
- Develop cross-functional expertise in a dynamic, tech-driven economy
- Gain hands-on experience through real-world projects and internships
- Build industry-ready portfolios and professional networks
- Attain global employability and academic mobility

### Eligibility

**For Indian Students:**
- Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)
- Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science / Engineering
- Bachelor’s Degree in Science / Commerce / Arts with Mathematics at 10+2 level
- Students without Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation can apply after qualifying the bridge course

**For Foreign Students:**
- Bachelor’s Degree (preferably BCA or equivalent)
- Proficiency in spoken and written English
- Certificate of equivalence from the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) for foreign education

**Target Audience:**
- Fresh graduates or final-year students aiming for IT/software careers
- Working professionals seeking to upskill or transition into IT
- Aspirants targeting roles in private/public sector organizations across IT, financial services, and more

**Target Audience:**
- Fresh graduates or final-year students seeking IT/software careers
- Working professionals aiming to upskill or transition into IT
- Aspirants targeting roles in IT, financial services, consulting, and more

### Admission Process

The admission process is outlined in a stepwise, visually guided format, often using numbered icons or a horizontal progress bar:

1. **Select Your Program:** Choose the MCA specialization that aligns with your career goals.
2. **Complete Your Application:** Fill out the online application form with personal, academic, and professional details.
3. **Upload Documents:** Submit scanned copies of academic certificates, ID proof, and photographs.
4. **Pay Program Fees:** Make a secure payment via credit/debit card, net banking, or UPI.
5. **Submit & Register:** Finalize your application and submit it for review.
6. **Await Enrollment Details:** Receive confirmation and further instructions via email.

A note clarifies that **no entrance exam is required**; admissions are based on merit and first-come, first-served basis. The process is designed to be fully online, with support available via chat, email, or phone.
